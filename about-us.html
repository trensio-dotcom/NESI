About Us

Health Education. Stronger Communities.

Who We Are

The Health Independence and Nutrition Education Initiative (HINEI) is a community-focused organization committed to preventing noncommunicable diseases through nutrition education, health promotion and community empowerment. We believe that lasting health independence starts with accessible knowledge and practical skills.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and communities with the knowledge, skills, and resources to take charge of their own health through nutrition education, while raising awareness of noncommunicable diseases.

Our Vision

A world where every person has the knowledge and confidence to achieve lasting health independence, and where communities are resilient against the growing burden of noncommunicable diseases.

Our Values

We are driven by integrity, community empowerment, scientific evidence, and a deep commitment to equity and compassion in health education.

Our Objectives

  • Improve nutrition knowledge and awareness.
  • Promote healthy eating habits and balanced diets.
  • Raise awareness of NCDs and their risk factors.
  • Encourage physical activity and healthy lifestyles.
  • Build community capacity for informed health decisions.
  • Collaborate with partners to promote preventive health.
  • Develop sustainable, evidence-based programmes.
